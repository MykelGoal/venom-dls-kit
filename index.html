How to Import Your Kit

Get your custom kit into DLS 26 in about 2 minutes.

1

Get your link

Order a kit or use the designer — you'll receive a direct .png import link for your design.

2

Unlock imports

One-time 300-diamond unlock in DLS: My Club → Customise → Kit → Custom Kit. (Per account, not per kit.)

3

Paste the URL

Paste your kit link into the importer field and press Confirm. The game wraps it onto your players automatically.

4

Wear it

Your full kit — jersey, shorts & socks — appears on your squad. No diamonds needed after the one-time unlock.
